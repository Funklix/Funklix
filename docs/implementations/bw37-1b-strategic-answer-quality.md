## Root causes and changes

- Raw authorized Brand Core/snapshot and validated Canvas were available, but aggregate findings lacked attribution and applicability. `api/_ai-brain-evidence.js` adds an Advice-only source guide, exact audience/tone groups with IDs/titles, short field quotations, actual selected-node incoming/outgoing endpoints and full-Canvas CTA/trust counts/IDs. Repeated titles do not merge node identities. Direct edges do not imply strategic dependencies. The helper never retrieves additional knowledge or alters original context.
- The index prioritizes the selected node, direct neighbors, then remaining Canvas order. It includes at most 24 node quotation entries with a 32,000-character duplication allowance (including label/title costs), exact 180-character prefix quotations and explicit excerpt/omission disclosure. Audience/tone groups cover indexed nodes; diagnostic scope counts and direct-neighbor IDs cover the whole validated Canvas. Full raw context remains supplied. Final serialized messages, including the index and policies, use the existing token budget and bounded 422 behavior; Learning statements are never split.
- Trusted Advice policy now asks for actual source/key/node/field/phrase evidence, separates observations from interpretations, assumptions and unknowns, and prioritizes positioning/audience/offering/constraints over archetype/tone. Review selects a concrete first adjustment and trade-off; Explore asks for distinct mechanisms/expression/action; Gaps checks applicability. All five modes, proportional replies and historical ordinal referents remain intact. Canonical identity and saved campaign constraints have scoped authority; nested drafts and module acceptance do not become approved Learnings.
- Existing diagnostics are retained verbatim and qualified alongside counterevidence. Top-level/social CTA is visible even when the aggregate rule misses it; preview need not be an action; missing LP trust is inapplicable without an LP. Label variation is not established strategic conflict, and missing five-stage coverage is not automatically a campaign defect.
- Client request identity serializes the exact same Canvas projection as dispatch, closing the top-level CTA omission and covering nested fields without a second field list. Account/Board/load/edit/selection checks, stale suppression and current-context retry remain intact.
- The existing text-only DOM formatter preserves bounded decimal ordered-list starts and per-item source ordinals, including resumed/discontinuous lists. Actual CR/LF normalization and literal backslash escapes are preserved. No CSS/layout changes or Markdown library.

## Semantic evaluation limits

Mocks establish deterministic evidence, governance and lifecycle contracts, not live recommendation quality. No paid model calls or production data operations were performed. The test fixture describes fictional creator workshops, independent makers wanting format choice, a conflicting historical partner campaign, an awareness-only constraint, repeated node titles, deliberate audience/tone variation and mixed-language follow-ups. It is not an Appics record.

The Section I rubric remains the review gate for live relevance, evidence precision, prioritization, creativity, uncertainty and actionability. There are no comparable baseline/candidate answer captures to score, and no two-human semantic review is claimed. For Review Strategy, reviewers must verify an actual node/field/phrase and justified first edit; for Find Gaps, check intentional segments, missing CTA fields versus copy and no-LP trust applicability; for Explore Ideas, verify a supplied product mechanism, audience need, concrete action and trade-off. Apply the same six dimensions to General/Evaluate and EN/DE follow-ups, marking creativity N/A where appropriate. Record exact sentences and resolve grounding disagreements against the fixture; do not average away fabricated facts. The three reported Appics cases remain unverified without authorized source payloads and complete answers.

Remaining limits: the provider may still ignore guidance; the index is partial; browser projection can omit/truncate native fields; native goals, shared diagnostic parity, Learning ranking, coverage/truncation contracts and output completion remain excluded follow-ups. Current boundary revalidation cannot retract already dispatched context. No schema/migration, provider/model, authorization, Learning selection, automatic write or explicit Content-node handoff changes. No merge or deployment.
